## 🎬 Production Notes

### Equipment Needed
- iPhone 13+ (sufficient for vertical video)
- Tripod or desk stand
- Natural lighting (no ring light = more authentic)
- CapCut or TikTok native editing

### Post-Production
- Captions: 50% French, 50% English (subtitles, not overdub)
- Music: Trending TikTok sounds (algorithm boost) + voice-over
- Transitions: Quick cuts (3-5s per scene)
- Color grading: Match MentalClear brand (indigo/purple tints, minimal)

### Legal/Compliance
- ✅ No medical claims ("cure," "treat," "diagnose")
- ✅ Use "for wellness tracking" language
- ✅ Disclose: "Built by someone in tech who burned out"
- ✅ Privacy: No real user data in videos (use mock/permission)
